Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Internet in One Word** — one word for the
whole internet. Funniest and most fitting wins, and this word has done both
jobs at once: it made me laugh, then it made me nod, then it made me check
the time and realize I'd been "just checking one thing" for two hours.

## The word

**scrollhole** — 10 characters.

You open the internet to check one thing. Three hours later you surface
somewhere you never meant to go, holding nothing, remembering nothing, and
already reaching for the next scroll. That is not a bug in the internet. That
*is* the internet: a hole you fall into by scrolling — and the worst part is,
nobody pushed you in. You jumped, happily, thumb first.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, fully deterministic) writes the
word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. That's the whole trick — no network,
no secrets, no files copied anywhere. Ten characters in, one word out, zero
excuses. If only the internet itself were this honest about what it does to
your afternoon.

```sh
node main.ts
```
